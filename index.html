01 · Project overview

What is AfyaSmart AI?

A digital health guidance concept designed to improve access to preliminary health information.

AfyaSmart AI is a proposed digital system that uses Artificial Intelligence to support the delivery of preliminary health guidance. It is intended to give users a place to report symptoms, answer simple follow-up questions and access general health education.

For example, a person experiencing fever, headache, cough or abdominal pain may not know whether to monitor the symptoms, contact a health professional or seek urgent care. AfyaSmart aims to help organise the information they provide and explain possible next steps in clear language.

The project focuses on accessibility, understandable information, symptom awareness and encouraging appropriate use of professional healthcare services.

02 · The challenge

Why is AfyaSmart needed?

People may face barriers when trying to understand health concerns and decide what to do next.

CHALLENGE 01

Limited access to timely information

Some people may have difficulty finding understandable health information when a concern first appears.

CHALLENGE 02

Uncertainty about symptoms

People may not know what information about their symptoms is important or whether they should seek care.

CHALLENGE 03

Distance and time barriers

Travel, work, family responsibilities and distance from health facilities can make it harder to seek advice promptly.

CHALLENGE 04

Confusing health information

Information found online may be difficult to understand or may not come from reliable sources.

CHALLENGE 05

Delayed decisions

Without clear guidance, a person may delay contacting a qualified health worker when assessment is needed.

CHALLENGE 06

Need for health awareness

People benefit from clear education about prevention, warning signs and appropriate healthcare options.

Preliminary guidance

Warning-sign awareness

Health education

05 · Project direction

To develop a digital system powered by Artificial Intelligence that provides preliminary health guidance and helps users access understandable health information and make informed decisions about seeking medical care.

09 · Trust and responsibility

Safety, privacy and limitations

Health technology must be clear about what it can and cannot do.

AfyaSmart AI is not a doctor and does not provide a confirmed diagnosis.

The website prototype demonstrates a possible user experience. Its sample symptom guidance is not a clinically validated AI model and should not be used to make medical decisions or delay treatment.

  • Users should seek advice from qualified healthcare professionals for medical concerns.
  • Possible emergency symptoms require immediate medical attention; users should not wait for a website response.
  • Before real-world launch, health content and triage logic need review by qualified medical professionals and appropriate testing.
  • Any collection of personal or health information must be protected through appropriate privacy, security and data-handling practices.
  • The contact form in the current demonstration does not send messages to a server.

11 · Frequently asked questions

Questions about AfyaSmart

Quick explanations of the project and its purpose.

Does AfyaSmart AI diagnose diseases?

No. It is designed for preliminary health information and guidance. It does not confirm a disease or replace a qualified healthcare professional.

Who can use AfyaSmart?

The proposed platform is intended for people seeking general health information, including community members and caregivers. Appropriate safeguards and professional advice remain important, especially for children and vulnerable people.

Can AfyaSmart replace a hospital visit?

No. It cannot examine a patient, run tests or provide treatment. Users should seek medical care when needed, and urgent warning signs require immediate attention.

Is the current website connected to a real AI medical system?

No. The current page is a project information website. Any demonstration symptom checker should be treated as a prototype, not a validated medical AI service.

What makes the project useful?

Its intended value is bringing symptom reporting, understandable preliminary information, health education and encouragement to seek professional care into one accessible digital experience.
